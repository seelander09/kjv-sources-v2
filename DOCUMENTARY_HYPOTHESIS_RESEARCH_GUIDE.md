# Documentary Hypothesis Research Guide
## Advanced AI-Powered Biblical Research Platform

### 🎯 **Overview**

This comprehensive research system provides PhD-level analysis of the Documentary Hypothesis using advanced AI capabilities. The system integrates your wiki_markdown source data, Weaviate collections, and Scriptural Truth content to provide unprecedented research capabilities.

### 🔬 **Available Research Tools**

#### 1. **Source Distribution Analysis**
- **Function**: `analyze_source_distribution`
- **Purpose**: Analyze the distribution of J, E, P, D, R sources across biblical books
- **Example Queries**:
  - "Analyze the distribution of J and P sources in Genesis"
  - "Show me the source distribution across all Pentateuch books"
  - "Compare the prevalence of Priestly source in Leviticus vs Numbers"

#### 2. **Parallel Passage Detection**
- **Function**: `find_parallel_passages`
- **Purpose**: Find parallel passages across different documentary sources
- **Example Queries**:
  - "Find parallel passages about creation across all sources"
  - "Show me all passages about covenant making in J, E, and D sources"
  - "Identify parallel sacrifice narratives in different sources"

#### 3. **Theological Source Comparison**
- **Function**: `compare_source_theology`
- **Purpose**: Compare theological themes and characteristics between sources
- **Example Queries**:
  - "Compare the theology of J and E sources regarding divine revelation"
  - "Analyze the differences between P and D source approaches to law"
  - "Compare how J, E, and P sources handle covenant theology"

#### 4. **Comprehensive Research Reports**
- **Function**: `generate_research_report`
- **Purpose**: Generate detailed research reports on specific topics
- **Example Queries**:
  - "Generate a research report on creation narratives in the Documentary Hypothesis"
  - "Create a comprehensive analysis of covenant theology across all sources"
  - "Produce a detailed report on priestly rituals and their theological significance"

#### 5. **Visual Analysis Data**
- **Function**: `create_visual_analysis`
- **Purpose**: Create data for charts and graphs
- **Example Queries**:
  - "Create a visual analysis of source distribution patterns"
  - "Generate theological theme distribution charts"
  - "Show me redaction pattern visualizations"

#### 6. **Source Characteristic Analysis**
- **Function**: `search_source_characteristics`
- **Purpose**: Search for specific characteristics within sources
- **Example Queries**:
  - "Search for divine name usage patterns in the J source"
  - "Find ritual elements in the P source"
  - "Identify narrative style differences in E source passages"

#### **Color-Coded Source System**
- **J (Jahwist)**: Navy Blue (#000088) - Anthropomorphic God, vivid storytelling
- **E (Elohist)**: Teal (#008888) - Prophetic emphasis, divine messengers
- **P (Priestly)**: Olive Yellow (#888800) - Systematic organization, ritual focus
- **D (Deuteronomist)**: Black (#000000) - Covenant theology, law emphasis
- **R (Redactor)**: Maroon Red (#880000) - Editorial harmonization
