## Mathematical Methodology

### Distance Metrics Used:
1. **Cosine Similarity**: Measures angle between frequency vectors
2. **Jensen-Shannon Distance**: Symmetric KL divergence for probability distributions
3. **Sentence Length Similarity**: Normalized difference in average sentence lengths

### Features Analyzed:
- Word frequency distributions (unigrams)
- Function word usage patterns
- Archaic KJV word frequencies
- Bigram and trigram patterns
- Syntactic patterns (sentence length, punctuation)
- Capitalization patterns
